# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A central entry point to OctoAcme’s project management guidance.

## Project management processes at a glance

OctoAcme manages cross-functional work through an iterative lifecycle. Initiation validates the business need, desired outcomes, stakeholders, risks, resources, and whether to proceed. Planning turns approved initiatives into prioritized, estimated backlogs with acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, milestones, and an initial test plan. During execution, teams deliver in small increments, track work on a project board, coordinate regularly, and manage risks and dependencies. Release preparation checks readiness, CI and security results, release notes, smoke tests, and rollback plans; deployment is followed by verification and stakeholder communication. Teams close each sprint, release, or milestone with a retrospective and tracked improvement actions.

Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, dependencies, and communications. Product Managers define outcomes, prioritize the backlog, and measure success. Developers build, test, and review changes; QA validates quality and acceptance; stakeholders provide input and approvals. Regular team and stakeholder updates, demos, measurable success signals, and clear escalation paths support transparency and timely blocker resolution.

Quality is integrated throughout delivery: use unit tests for new logic, integration tests where applicable, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows, and manual QA when needed. Run automated tests, linting, and security scans in CI; keep pull requests small where possible, linked to issues and acceptance criteria, and reviewed according to team policy. Before and after deployment, follow readiness, staging, smoke-test, rollback, and verification practices in the release guide.
