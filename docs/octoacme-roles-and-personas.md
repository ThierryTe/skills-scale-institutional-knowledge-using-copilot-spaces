# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsor

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors provide strategic sponsorship, funding alignment, and executive support for the initiative. They help ensure that the project remains aligned with business priorities and that critical trade-offs are resolved at the right level.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm business case and strategic value
- Sponsor scope trade-offs and resourcing decisions
- Support go/no-go decisions and milestone approvals
- Escalate major business or organizational risks
- Align cross-functional stakeholders with organizational priorities

### Goals
- Ensure the initiative delivers measurable business value
- Maintain executive alignment and sponsorship
- Reduce friction across teams and stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Executive briefings and milestone reviews
- Escalation updates on blockers or strategic dependencies
- Decisions on priority, resourcing, and trade-offs

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business needs into clear requirements and process understanding. They help ensure the team builds the right thing by documenting workflows, success criteria, and business context.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and clarify business requirements from stakeholders
- Define user workflows and process constraints
- Support acceptance criteria and requirement traceability
- Identify gaps between current-state and target-state operations
- Facilitate alignment between product, engineering, and operations

### Goals
- Improve requirement clarity and stakeholder alignment
- Reduce misunderstandings between business and technical teams
- Support delivery of solutions that meet user and business needs

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews and workshops
- Requirements reviews with Product Managers and developers
- Acceptance and validation discussions with QA

---

## UX/UI Designer / User Researcher

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers shape usability, accessibility, and customer experience. They ensure that solutions are intuitive, user-centered, and supported by evidence from research and testing.

### Responsibilities
- Research customer needs, behaviors, and pain points
- Design user journeys, wireframes, and interface concepts
- Validate accessibility and usability with stakeholders and users
- Collaborate with Product Managers on prioritization and outcomes
- Partner with developers and QA on implementation and usability checks

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and usability
- Reduce friction in user workflows and onboarding
- Align product experience with business goals and user needs

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and usability testing sessions
- Collaboration with Product Managers and developers on features
- Feedback loops with QA and stakeholders during validation

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide design quality, technical direction, and system-level decisions. They help balance product goals with platform health, scalability, reliability, and long-term maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction and architectural guardrails
- Review design decisions and technical trade-offs
- Identify integration, dependency, and risk concerns
- Guide developers on implementation quality and standards
- Coordinate with QA and operations on readiness and observability

### Goals
- Maintain a sustainable technical foundation
- Improve system quality, scalability, and maintainability
- Reduce delivery risk from architecture and integration issues

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical planning sessions
- Dependency and risk discussions with Project Managers and developers
- Coordination with QA and operations on technical readiness

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the readiness, scheduling, and communication of deployments. They help ensure that releases happen smoothly, with clear ownership, verification, and rollback planning.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release calendars and deployment windows
- Validate release readiness across engineering, QA, and operations
- Maintain release notes and stakeholder communications
- Support rollout, rollback, and incident coordination when needed
- Track post-release verification and follow-up actions

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and operational surprises
- Improve consistency of deployment practices
- Keep stakeholders informed before and after releases

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and Go/No-Go check-ins
- Status updates with Project Managers, QA, and stakeholders
- Coordination with support or operations during rollout and follow-up

---

## Operations / Site Reliability / Support Representative

### Role Summary
Operations, Site Reliability, and Support representatives ensure that systems are reliable, observable, and supportable in production. They help teams prepare for real-world operations and customer impact.

### Responsibilities
- Review production readiness, monitoring, and alerting needs
- Support incident response and service restoration plans
- Provide feedback on reliability, performance, and supportability
- Share production signals and customer issues with the team
- Help define operational requirements before release

### Goals
- Improve service reliability and customer trust
- Reduce operational risk and incident impact
- Support a smooth handoff from delivery to production support

### Typical Communication
- Readiness checks before release
- Post-release monitoring and incident coordination
- Feedback loops with Product Managers, developers, and PMs on service quality

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These additional roles help clarify accountability, dependencies, and handoffs across initiation, planning, execution, release, and continuous improvement.
